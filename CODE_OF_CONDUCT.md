# Code of Conduct

We want DevOrch to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussion on topic and credit other people's work.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing threads, or spamming.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits) and when someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and ban people who do not follow these
standards. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer at the address listed in the `authors`
field of `pyproject.toml`. Reports are treated confidentially.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
